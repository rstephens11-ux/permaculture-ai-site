Privacy

Short, and true as of the date below.

Your photo

It is sent to our own machine, drawn on, and not stored. It isn't written to disk, it isn't used to train anything, and it isn't sent to any cloud AI service — the drawing is made by a model running on the workshop computer. Our logs record the size of the file and how long the render took. They never record the image.

Your email, if you give it

It goes into one file that we keep, and that's it. Nothing is being sent yet — no newsletter exists until The Preservation Run does. When it does, you get one email, and one click removes you. We don't sell or share the list.

What we don't do

  • No analytics scripts, no advertising pixels, no third-party trackers.
  • No accounts, no passwords, no profiles.
  • No cookies beyond whatever your browser keeps for itself.

What we do count

How many plans were made today and how many people asked (so the machine doesn't get swamped). Those are counters, not records of you.
