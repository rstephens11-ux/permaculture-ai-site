# content/site-copy.md — every string that matters, in one place

Edit here, then move the change into the page. The point of this file is that you should never have to
hunt through HTML to change a sentence.

## Voice

Plain, dry, Maine. Short sentences. No growth-hacker language, no "unlock", no "supercharge", no
exclamation marks. If a line sounds like a landing page, rewrite it until it sounds like a person in a
workshop who is slightly busy. Understate. The tool is free and it tells the truth about being busy;
that's the personality.

## The one-line promise

> Plan your patch from a single photo.

Alternates, if the main one wears out:
- One photo in. One planting plan out.
- Photograph the yard. See the beds.

## The fusion line (this is the whole business)

> Forty pounds of tomatoes land in ten days. That is the week that breaks people. The planting plan is
> the easy half — the hard half is August.

## Free tool

- Button: **Grow me a plan 🌱**
- Sub-button: Free. No account. We don't keep your photo.
- Busy machine: You keep your place in line and the page says so. Nothing is lost.
- Forge asleep: The greenhouse is closed — the machine that draws these is offline. Your place is held.
- One a day: One free plan per person per day — the machine only has so many hours.
- Too big: That photo is over 6 MB. Most phones can shrink it when they send it.
- Wrong type: That isn't a JPEG, PNG or WebP. A photo straight off the phone is perfect.
- Failed render: That one didn't make it. Nothing was kept. Try again?
- Expired in line: This one sat too long — the machine never freed up. Send the photo again.

## Disclaimer (must appear with every result)

> Concept sketch only — not construction drawings, not a survey, and not a guarantee of what will grow.

## The Preservation Run

- Price line: **$29 when it's ready.**
- Not-yet line: Not yet — this page is the list. One email when it's real, and nothing else in between.
- Waitlist button: **Join the list**
- Waitlist confirmation: You're on the list. Nothing else will land in your inbox until The Preservation
  Run is real — and then it's one email.

## Footer honesty line

> The drawing is a concept sketch. Not construction drawings, not a survey, and not a promise about what
> will grow.

## The gate (one free, then five a week)

- Allowance line, free: **Your free plan — the first one's on us.**
- Allowance line, unlocked: **N of 5 plans left this week.**
- Allowance line, used up: **You've used this week's plans (5). They come back in N days.**
- Gate heading: **That was your free one.**
- Gate button: **Unlock five a week**
- Gate body: Pop your email in and you get five plans a week. One email when The Preservation Run is
  ready, and nothing else in between.
- Server sentence when the gate blocks: *That was your free plan. Pop your email in and you'll get five a
  week from here.* / *You've used all five for this week. They come back in a few days.*

## The zip code

- Field label: **your zip code — this is the one we need**
- Why: We look up your real frost dates from NOAA's 1991-2020 climate records, and pick crops that will
  finish in your season. Nothing is stored about where you are.
- Refusal: A five-digit zip code, please — that's what gives you local frost dates.
- Honest limit, when a ZIP resolves: *Estimated, measured at the nearest weather station, about N miles
  away — a cold hollow or a hilltop can differ by a week.*
- Honest limit, when it doesn't: *No frost record for that zip — the dates below are general, not local.*

## Things never to write

- Any preserving time, temperature or pressure. Point at the USDA/NCHFP guide instead.
- Any frost date stated as fact. Always "confirm your own".
- Any claim that the tool is "AI-powered permaculture design" in a way that implies a qualified designer.
- Anything about the machine that isn't true. If it's a 3090 in a workshop, say workshop.
