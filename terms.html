Plain language, because that's the only kind worth reading.

What the tool gives you

A concept layout of your space, drawn to scale from the numbers you give us, and a dated season plan to go with it. It is a sketch of intent, plus an illustrated version of your photo for looks. It is not construction drawings, a survey, a soil test, an engineering document, or a promise about what will grow where you live.

Dates are estimates

Harvest windows are calculated from typical days-to-maturity for common garden varieties and a typical last-frost window for your area. Your soil, your varieties, your slope and your season will move them. Confirm your own frost dates.

Growing and preserving food is your call

We don't give preserving times, temperatures or pressures, and we don't diagnose plant problems. For anything you intend to eat, follow current guidance from the USDA/NCHFP Complete Guide to Home Canning and your state extension service. If a step here conflicts with that, they're right and we're wrong.

Availability

The tool runs on one machine in one workshop, so it is sometimes busy or asleep, and it may have a daily limit. We can change or stop offering it at any time. Nothing you send is a contract.
