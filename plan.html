what happens next
  1. Your zip gives us your frost dates and your season length, from NOAA records.
  2. Your photo goes to our own machine — nothing is sent to a cloud AI.
  3. Back comes your plan drawn to scale — what goes in each bed and how much, the season in eight dated phases, and the window each crop lands in.

If the machine is asleep or mid-render, you keep your place in line and the page says so. Nothing is lost.

before you press it

The plan is a concept layout, not construction drawings, and the dates are estimates from the nearest weather station — a cold hollow two miles from a hilltop can differ by a week. Confirm your own frost dates before you plant anything tender.
