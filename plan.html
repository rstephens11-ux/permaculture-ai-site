what happens next
  1. Your photo goes to our own machine — nothing is sent to a cloud AI.
  2. It draws the beds, paths and water onto your photo and numbers them.
  3. You get the drawing, five steps in order, and the window each crop lands in.

If the machine is asleep or mid-render, you keep your place in line and the page says so. Nothing is lost.
