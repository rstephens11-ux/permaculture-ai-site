# Permaculture AI — site sketches (design pick)

Three throwaway directions for the same page. Same content in all three:
hero → photo drop → sample plan (5 numbered steps + preservation window) → The Preservation Run waitlist.

Screenshots: `~/.hermes/cache/scratch/pa-sketches/out/<variant>-top.png` (desktop hero),
`-full.png` (whole page), `-phone.png` (390x900).

| | 001-almanac-editorial | 001-workbench-tool-first | 001-garden-gate-playful |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stance | printed almanac / seed packet | dashboard tool panel | friendly garden shop |
| Ground | warm paper, serif, thin rules | dark slate, mono labels, single green accent | cream + leaf green + carrot orange, chunky 2px borders |
| Emphasis | trust and voice | the tool, above the fold | warmth and shareability |
| Density | airy | dense, two-pane | medium |
| Tool is | a card in the flow | the whole left pane | a boxed widget |
| Queue/downtime | one line of small print | its own panel (position, est. wait, progress) | a note under the button |
| Strong at | feeling like someone who knows | making the machine legible; best for a busy/queued GPU | beginners, phone screens, screenshots people pass around |
| Weak at | buries the tool; thinnest on mechanics | coldest; the least "gardening" | risks reading as a toy |

**My take:** the workbench stance is the only one that makes the real constraint (one home GPU, one
plan at a time, a queue) visible and honest instead of hiding it — and that constraint is permanent
with the forge-only engine. The almanac stance has the best voice, and it is also the direction that
would age best as a *business* rather than a tool page. The playful one is the most shareable and the
weakest on credibility for people who actually can tomatoes.

Best combination, if you want one: **almanac's voice and type on the workbench's bones** — paper
palette, mono only for the machine-facing bits (queue, job, engine), tool above the fold.
