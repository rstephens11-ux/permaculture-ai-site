The Preservation Run

A season's worth of putting food up, arranged so the week everything ripens doesn't turn into a panic.

$29 when it's ready. Not yet — this page is the list. One email when it's real, and nothing else in between.

What's inside

PartWhat it does
The glut triage table Crop × how much you've got × how many days until it's loss × what to do. The whole book in one page for the mornings you can't think.
The methods, in order of effort Freezing, drying, root cellaring, fermenting, water-bath and pressure canning — what each one is actually good for, and what it ruins.
Safe processing, sourced Where the real numbers live and how to read them. Times, pressures and temperatures come from the USDA/NCHFP Complete Guide to Home Canning, not from a blog and not from us.
The worked week Forty pounds of tomatoes, hour by hour, from the counter to the shelf. Including the boring parts: the blanching, the labels, the floor.
Worksheets Printable pages for the freezer inventory, the jar log and the root cellar — plus a small spreadsheet that totals what you've put up by month.

What it isn't

  • Not a canning-safety manual. Those exist, they're free, and they're authoritative — we point you at them and mean it.
  • Not a recipe book. You have recipe books. This is the schedule that tells you which one to open tonight.
  • Not a subscription and not an app. One file you keep.
